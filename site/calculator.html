    The card behind the numbers

    MultipleConfidenceFlagsShare of firms80% range around the point estimate

    Ranges are point × centre ÷ factor to point × centre × factor. The centre (× 1.03 for EV/EBITDA, × 1.06 for EV/Sales) corrects the average gap between market value and the peer median.

    Evidence. 26,776 US non-financial firm-years with market value of at least $100M, FY2016–2025 (FactSet). The peer 25th–75th percentile range contained market value about 41% of the time in every period. Fitted on FY2016–2019 only, this card's ranges contained it 72% / 70% (EV/EBITDA) and 79% / 78% (EV/Sales) of the time in FY2020–2023 and FY2024–2025. EV/EBITDA ranges ran short of 80% in later years for every method tested, so treat the EV/EBITDA range as a floor. Limits. Listed firms, trailing multiples, market value as the benchmark. Tested with five different ways of choosing peers; coverage held across them. Not tested on private companies or forward multiples. The card was built after the pre-registered tests, so its out-of-sample figures are exploratory. Code, protocol and reports: github.com/joonha9/comps-range-lab
